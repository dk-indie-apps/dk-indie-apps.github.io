## Frequently asked questions

### I bought a word pack but it shows as locked

Tap "Восстановить покупки" (Restore Purchases) in the store or in Settings. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used for the purchase. Purchases are one-time and tied to your Apple ID, so they can always be restored — even after reinstalling the app or switching to a new iPhone.

### I want a refund for a word pack

In-app purchase refunds are handled entirely by Apple. Visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) and request a refund directly. We have no access to your payment information and cannot issue refunds ourselves.

### My unfinished game disappeared

An unfinished game is saved automatically and survives app restarts — the main menu shows "Продолжить игру" (Continue game). The game is removed only when a team wins or when you explicitly confirm starting a new game.

### Words started repeating

The app remembers which words you have already seen and only starts over once every word in the selected difficulty and packs has been shown. Buying additional word packs adds fresh words. Word history is stored locally and resets if you uninstall the app.

### The app crashed

We'd love to fix it. Please email us with:

- Your iPhone model
- Your iOS version
- What happened just before the crash

### How do I disable sounds or vibration?

Settings screen (gear icon on the main menu) — toggle sounds and haptics independently.
