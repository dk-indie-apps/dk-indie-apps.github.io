## Frequently asked questions

### My high scores or unlocked facts disappeared

Scores, unlocked facts, and settings are stored locally on your device. If you uninstall and reinstall the app, they reset. The app does not sync to iCloud.

### What is the maths question before some screens?

That is a parental gate. Nerpa Tales is made for children, so purchases and links that leave the app first ask a question designed for grown-ups. If a child taps them, the gate simply closes.

### I want a refund for my tip

In-app purchase refunds are handled entirely by Apple. Visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) and request a refund directly. We have no access to your payment information and cannot issue refunds ourselves.

### Do donations to seal charities go through the app?

No. The "Support real nerpas" screen only links to a conservation organisation's own website, where any donation happens directly between you and them. We never handle donations and receive no part of them.

### How do I change the language?

Settings screen (from the main menu) — tap the Language row to switch between English and Russian instantly.

### How do I disable music or sound effects?

Settings screen — toggle sound or haptics independently.

### The app crashed

We'd love to fix it. Please email us with:

- Your iPhone model
- Your iOS version
- Which mini-game you were playing
- What happened just before the crash
