---

## Frequently asked questions

### My progress or collection disappeared

Quiz progress, your collection, and stats are stored locally on your device. If you uninstall and reinstall the app, they reset. The app does not sync to iCloud.

### I bought the full version but the quiz is still locked

Open the **Settings** tab and tap **Restore Purchases**. If it's still locked, make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to buy it. If the problem persists, email us.

### I want a refund

In-app purchase refunds are handled entirely by Apple. Visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) and request a refund directly. We have no access to your payment information and cannot issue refunds ourselves.

### I think an answer or attribution is wrong

We'd love to fix it. Email us the question (a screenshot helps) and what you think the correct answer is. We verify artwork attributions against public art databases and update the content in app releases.

### The app crashed

Please email us with:

- Your iPhone model
- Your iOS version
- What you were doing just before the crash

### When does the Daily Challenge reset?

A new Daily Challenge appears every day. The same date always gives the same ten questions.
