GitHub Pages site that hosts the public landing, privacy policy, and support pages for all apps shipped under the DK Indie Apps account.

## What it does

Each app gets a subfolder (e.g. `knight-fight/`) with privacy and support pages, linked from a single landing `index.md`. Lives under `~/Projects/indie-apps/`, which is configured to use the indie git identity automatically.
