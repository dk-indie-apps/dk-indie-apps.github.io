## Frequently asked questions

### My moments disappeared

Your moments and settings are stored locally on your device. If you uninstall and reinstall the app, they reset. The app does not sync to iCloud.

### I'm not getting reminders

Open **Settings → Reminders** in the app and make sure reminders are enabled and the times are set. If iOS notifications were denied, the app will show a banner linking you to the system Settings, where you can allow notifications for 20 Peas.

### How do I change the language?

**Settings → Language.** 20 Peas is available in 11 languages, independent of your device language.

### How do I turn off the brush sound?

**Settings → Sound.** Toggle it off and the app stays silent when you log a moment.

### The home screen widget isn't updating

The widget refreshes periodically and resets at midnight. If it looks out of date, log a moment in the app — the widget updates right away.

### I want a refund for my tip

In-app purchase refunds are handled entirely by Apple. Visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) and request a refund directly. We have no access to your payment information and cannot issue refunds ourselves.

### The app crashed

We'd love to fix it. Please email us with:

- Your iPhone model
- Your iOS version
- What you were doing just before the crash
